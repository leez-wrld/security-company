RISK ASSESSMENT

Comprehensive evaluation of security vulnerabilities with detailed recommendations for improvements

Professional Security Analysis

Our risk assessment services provide a thorough evaluation of your property's security vulnerabilities and potential threats. Our team of security experts conducts comprehensive assessments to identify weaknesses in your current security measures and develop strategic recommendations to mitigate risks.


A professional risk assessment is the foundation of any effective security program. By identifying vulnerabilities before they can be exploited, you can proactively address security gaps and allocate resources efficiently. Our assessments consider physical security, technological systems, operational procedures, and human factors.


All of our risk assessments are conducted by certified security professionals with extensive experience across various industries. We use a methodical approach that combines industry best practices, regulatory compliance requirements, and innovative security solutions to provide you with actionable recommendations.

Physical Security Assessment

Evaluation of building perimeters, entry points, barriers, lighting, and other physical security measures to identify vulnerabilities.

Technical Systems Review

Analysis of surveillance, access control, alarm systems, and other security technologies to ensure optimal configuration and coverage.

Operational Security Audit

Review of security policies, procedures, and protocols to identify gaps and ensure alignment with industry best practices.

Threat Analysis

Identification and assessment of potential threats specific to your location, industry, and operations to prioritize security measures.

Frequently Asked Questions

How often should we conduct a security risk assessment?

We recommend conducting a comprehensive security risk assessment annually, as well as after any significant changes to your facility, operations, or when security incidents occur. Regular assessments ensure your security measures remain effective against evolving threats. For high-risk facilities or those in rapidly changing environments, more frequent assessments (semi-annually or quarterly) may be appropriate. We can help establish a customized assessment schedule based on your specific risk profile and industry requirements.

What does a risk assessment typically include?

Our comprehensive risk assessments typically include evaluation of physical security measures (perimeter security, barriers, lighting), technical systems (surveillance, access control, alarms), operational procedures (security policies, emergency response plans), and human factors (staff awareness, training). We also conduct threat analysis specific to your location and industry, review of past security incidents, compliance verification with relevant regulations, and testing of existing security systems. The assessment concludes with a detailed report containing prioritized recommendations and implementation strategies.

How long does a risk assessment take?

The duration of a risk assessment depends on the size and complexity of your facility, as well as the scope of the assessment. Typically, the on-site portion takes 1-3 days for average-sized facilities, while larger or more complex properties may require up to a week. Following the on-site assessment, our team generally requires 1-2 weeks to analyze findings and prepare the comprehensive report. We work with you to schedule the assessment with minimal disruption to your operations, including after-hours evaluations when necessary.

How do you prioritize security recommendations?

We prioritize recommendations based on a risk matrix that considers both the likelihood of a threat occurring and the potential impact if it does. High-likelihood, high-impact vulnerabilities receive the highest priority. We also consider implementation factors such as cost, complexity, and time required. Our recommendations are categorized as immediate action items (critical vulnerabilities requiring urgent attention), short-term improvements (to be implemented within 3-6 months), and long-term strategic enhancements. This approach allows you to address the most critical security gaps first while planning for comprehensive security improvements.

Can you help implement the recommended security improvements?

Yes, we offer comprehensive implementation services for all recommended security improvements. Our team can manage the entire process, from developing detailed specifications and selecting appropriate vendors to overseeing installation and testing of new security measures. We can also provide partial implementation support, such as developing RFPs, reviewing vendor proposals, or providing project management services. For clients who prefer to handle implementation internally, we offer consultation services to ensure proper execution of our recommendations. Our goal is to provide as much or as little support as you need to enhance your security posture.
