Frequently Asked Questions

What types of cameras do you offer?

We offer a wide range of camera types to suit different security needs, including dome cameras, bullet cameras, PTZ (pan-tilt-zoom) cameras, thermal cameras, and discreet pinhole cameras. Each type has specific advantages for different environments and security requirements. Our experts will recommend the best combination based on your property layout and security concerns.

How long is surveillance footage stored?

Storage duration depends on your specific requirements and system configuration. Typical storage periods range from 30 to 90 days, but we can customize retention periods based on your needs and compliance requirements. We offer both on-site storage solutions (DVRs/NVRs) and cloud-based options with various capacity levels to ensure you have access to historical footage when needed.

Can I access my surveillance system remotely?

Yes, all of our surveillance systems include remote access capabilities. You can view live and recorded footage from anywhere using a secure mobile app or web interface. Our systems support multi-device access with user permission controls, allowing you to grant specific access levels to different users. Remote access is secured with encryption and multi-factor authentication to protect your surveillance data.

Do you offer monitoring services for surveillance systems?

Yes, we provide professional monitoring services for surveillance systems. Our monitoring options include 24/7 live monitoring by security professionals, automated alert monitoring with notification services, and hybrid approaches. Our monitoring center can respond to alerts, verify threats using your camera feeds, and dispatch emergency services if necessary. We also offer scheduled virtual guard tours of your property using your camera system.

How often should surveillance systems be maintained?

We recommend quarterly maintenance checks for optimal performance, though specific schedules may vary based on your environment and system complexity. Our maintenance services include camera cleaning, alignment adjustments, firmware updates, storage management, and comprehensive system testing. We also offer maintenance contracts with priority service and discounted rates for repairs and upgrades.
